ZeusWatch is an open-source Android weather console for people who want to know where a forecast came from and whether other models agree. Core use needs no account, subscription, or API key. There are no ads or analytics.

SEE THE SECOND OPINION

Compare two locations side by side. Inspect Open-Meteo, ECMWF AIFS, MET Norway, and other configured sources. Provider-agreement and confidence views show where temperature or rain predictions begin to diverge.

BUILD YOUR WEATHER DASHBOARD

Choose and reorder 37 cards. Follow up to 72 hours of hourly weather, scan a compact 16-day outlook, review warnings, and tune units or alert thresholds. Cached forecasts remain visible when the network drops.

CHOOSE YOUR SOURCES

Select forecast, radar, air-quality, and alert providers independently. Radar choices include Windy, LibreWXR, RainViewer, and official NOAA/NWS views. Regional warning sources cover the United States, Europe, Canada, Japan, Hong Kong, Indonesia, and WMO-supported regions.

USE IT ACROSS ANDROID

The Wear OS companion includes forecasts, warnings, a tile, and complications. Eight home-screen widgets cover compact temperature views, hourly strips, daily forecasts, and saved cities. ZeusWatch also supports live wallpaper, Smartspacer, Gadgetbridge, shareable weather images, and read-only cached-data access for tools such as Tasker or KWGT.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT

ZeusWatch uses foreground-only location and stores saved places on the device. Optional provider keys are encrypted locally. The Google-free freenet build removes Firebase and Google Play Services dependencies.
