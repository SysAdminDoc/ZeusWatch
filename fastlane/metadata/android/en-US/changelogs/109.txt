A full visual rebuild of the phone app. Near-black backgrounds with a cyan accent, flat cards that stop competing with the data on them, and denser Hourly and Daily tables you can read at a glance. Hourly and Daily now keep the same toolbar as Today, so location, share, compare, and settings are always in the same place. Locations, Compare, Custom Alerts, and Settings got tighter layouts, the native radar controls were cleaned up, and the map picker opens on a saved location instead of the middle of nowhere. Touch targets stay at 48 dp and the whole thing was checked at 1.3x, 1.5x, and 1.8x text scaling.
