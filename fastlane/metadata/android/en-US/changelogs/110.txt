Windy is the radar default again on a fresh install, and it is the fallback when another provider cannot be reached. Your own radar choice is untouched. The radar card on Today now embeds the selected web provider directly instead of sending you to another screen, and native previews draw on OpenStreetMap now that the old watermarked base map is gone. Five data switches in Settings that did nothing were removed. Snowfall, storm potential, sunshine, golden hour, and outdoor score are still there, controlled from Home Cards like every other card. Your existing choices carry over.
